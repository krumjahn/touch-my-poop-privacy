Touch My Poop
Privacy Policy

Developer: Keith Rumjahn
Effective date: October 31, 2026
Applies to Touch My Poop version 2.0 and later (Android package com.rumjahn.touchmypoop).

No data collection or sharing

Touch My Poop is an offline game. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The game has no advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, in-app purchases, or third-party data collection SDKs. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or device identifiers.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your personal best, total successful touches, and sound preference locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer or any third party. It remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. The game does not offer cloud synchronization.

Children's privacy

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can mute sound in the game. To delete locally saved progress and settings, clear Touch My Poop's storage in Android settings or uninstall it.

Changes and contact

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated. For questions about this policy, contact Keith Rumjahn at rumjahn@gmail.com.
